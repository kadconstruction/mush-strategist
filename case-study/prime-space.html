PRIMESPACE INVESTMENTS

Industry: Real Estate & Investment
Platform: Instagram
Role: Social Media Manager

PrimeSpace Investments is a real estate investment brand focused on property opportunities, market insights and investment education.

My role focused on managing the brand's social media content and presentation through a strategy combining real estate marketing, investment education, market communication and premium visual storytelling.

The account was positioned as an investment-focused real estate media platform, rather than simply a page for advertising properties.

CHALLENGE

PrimeSpace Investments needed a structured digital presence capable of communicating real estate opportunities while also providing audiences with useful investment information.

STRATEGY

I developed the Instagram content strategy around real estate investment, property marketing, education and market positioning.

WHAT I DID

  • Developed the Instagram content strategy around real estate investment.
  • Created property listing and promotional content.
  • Developed content around investment opportunities and market analysis.
  • Created premium-looking property visuals for high-value listings.
  • Structured the account into content categories such as tips, deals, investments, market information and results.
  • Created educational content to make real estate investment information easier to understand.
  • Maintained a consistent premium visual identity.
  • Combined promotional content with educational and authority-building content.
  • Developed content designed to attract potential investors and property buyers.
  • Positioned the account as an investment-focused real estate media platform.

CONTENT APPROACH

The content strategy combined property marketing, investment education, market information and premium visual communication.

KEY IMPACT

I helped establish a structured content system combining property marketing, investment education, market positioning and premium visual communication.
