LA RÉSIDENCE DZ

Industry: Real Estate
Platform: Instagram
Role: Social Media Manager

La Résidence DZ is a real estate agency focused on property marketing and real estate services.

My role focused on managing and developing the brand's Instagram presence through property-focused content, visual presentation, audience engagement and strategic content planning.

The account was approached as a digital real estate showroom, designed to showcase properties, communicate services, maintain brand visibility and encourage prospective buyers to make inquiries.

CHALLENGE

The account needed to consistently present a large volume of real estate information in a way that remained visually appealing, organized and easy for the audience to understand.

STRATEGY

I developed the Instagram content strategy around property marketing, visual storytelling, audience engagement and real estate communication.

WHAT I DID

  • Developed the Instagram content strategy around property listings and real estate services.
  • Created property-focused content designed to capture attention quickly.
  • Structured content around villas, apartments, properties for sale and real estate services.
  • Developed branded property graphics and promotional creatives.
  • Organized Instagram Highlights around important services and property categories.
  • Used visual storytelling to make property listings more attractive and easier to understand.
  • Incorporated educational and advisory content alongside promotional posts.
  • Maintained consistency in branding across the Instagram grid.
  • Developed content designed to encourage prospective buyers to make inquiries.
  • Treated the account as a digital real estate showroom rather than simply a posting page.

CONTENT APPROACH

The content strategy combined property promotion, visual communication, education and brand presentation.

KEY IMPACT

I helped maintain a large-scale real estate social presence capable of showcasing properties, communicating services and keeping the brand consistently visible to its audience.
