URBANNEST PROPERTIES

Industry: Real Estate
Platform: Instagram
Role: Social Media Manager

UrbanNest Properties is a property and real estate investment brand focused on property listings, investment opportunities and market information.

My role focused on managing the brand's social media content and positioning through a structured content strategy combining property marketing, investment education, social proof and brand communication.

The account was positioned as more than a platform for advertising properties. The strategy was designed to make it a property discovery and investment information platform for audiences interested in real estate opportunities.

CHALLENGE

UrbanNest Properties needed a social media presence that could communicate both property opportunities and investment information without relying entirely on promotional content.

STRATEGY

I developed a content strategy centered around property marketing, investment education, information and social proof.

WHAT I DID

  • Developed a content strategy around property listings and investment education.
  • Created branded property marketing graphics.
  • Developed content around land sales and investment opportunities.
  • Structured the page around multiple content pillars including listings, investors, collaborations and client wins.
  • Created content designed to educate potential investors rather than simply advertise properties.
  • Maintained consistent visual branding across the account.
  • Used location, property and investment information to make listings more informative.
  • Incorporated social proof and client-focused content into the strategy.
  • Developed promotional creatives designed to generate inquiries.
  • Positioned the social media account as a property discovery and investment information platform.

CONTENT APPROACH

The content strategy balanced property promotion with education, information and credibility-building content.

KEY IMPACT

I helped build a structured content ecosystem combining property marketing, investor education, social proof and brand positioning.
