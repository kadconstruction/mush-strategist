About me

I’m Mujeeb Musbau, a Nigerian digital marketing and media professional working at the intersection of SEO, social media, content strategy, digital marketing, media production, public relations and communication.
Through my professional brand, MUSH STRATEGIST, I help businesses, organizations and personal brands build stronger digital presences, communicate their ideas effectively and connect with their audiences through purposeful digital strategy and creative content.
My approach goes beyond simply creating posts or managing social media pages. I look at the bigger picture — what a brand wants to communicate, who it needs to reach, how its audience behaves, and how digital platforms can be used to create a stronger and more consistent presence.
